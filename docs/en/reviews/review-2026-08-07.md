## PLO recalibration — score changes

The PLO hand-evaluation fixes (nut potential, dirty outs, board change) lowered the
WTSD baseline by 6–8 percentage points. Subsequent score adjustments:

### bot-category-scores.ts
- **LAG PLO**: raise scores back to the v0.7.8 level, postflop call scores increased
- **TAG PLO**: raise scores increased (good 6→14, strong 12→20), protection bonuses strengthened
- **Nit PLO**: fold scores increased, call scores reduced (FR WTSD 30.9% → 28.5%)
- **CS PLO**: minimal adjustments

### bot-action-scoring.ts
- PLO protection bonus: `{wet: 12, dry: 8}` → `{wet: 8, dry: 6}` (back to the original)
- PLO board-worse sensitivity: `0.6` → `0.4` (back to the original, in `scoreRaise` and `calculateRaiseTo`)

### simulation.ts — target adjustments
- **NLHE**: C-bet (LAG 80–90%→68–78%, Nit 45–58%→60–72%), AF caps (TAG 3.8, Nit 3.5, LAG 4.2)
- **PLO WTSD**: TAG [22–32], Nit [22–28], LAG [28–34], CS [28–45] — after PLO realism advice
- **PLO LAG AF**: cap from 6.0 to 4.2

### Known limits (v0.8.0)
- LAG FR WTSD 11.8% vs target 28–34% — needs pot-commitment logic
- Nit AF explodes when fold-pushing — needs dynamic fold thresholds
- PLO WTSD targets met for TAG/CS, borderline for Nit
