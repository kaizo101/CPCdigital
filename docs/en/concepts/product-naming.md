# Product Naming

Status: working notes; the final product name has not yet been decided.

As of: 9 August 2026

`CPCdigital` is an internal development and project name. The final product
name is decided at the naming checkpoint of v0.9.2, before packaging, release
candidate and broader communication begin.

## Candidate: CheckBack

Status: **Shortlist, no name decision**

### Why the name fits the product

- "Check back" is an established poker term: a player in position checks
  after having previously been checked to.
- The second reading "look back" fits hand replay, session history, decision
  analysis and the later learning layer.
- The name is short, internationally pronounceable and less casino-like than
  many direct poker terms.
- `Check` and `Back` offer good visual hooks for word mark, check symbol,
  back-jump or replay motif.

Possible positionings:

- **CheckBack** — *Poker that remembers.*
- **CheckBack** — *Play the player.*
- **CheckBack** — *Every hand tells a story.*
- **CheckBack Poker** as a more unambiguous, better classifiable variant

### Risks and open questions

- In poker the term is technically generic. That makes organic search and the
  unambiguous assignment of a stand-alone word mark harder.
- Outside poker, "check back" can sound like "come back later" and thereby
  sound unfinished or passive.
- **Checkback International** has used the name for many years for European
  background checks and its own software platform:
  <https://checkback.co.uk/about-us/>.
- A Japanese review/collaboration product is also called **CheckBack**:
  <https://rayout-inc.com/downloads/checkback>.
- Further cashback, loyalty and software offers with the same name exist in
  other markets. That increases the need for a check despite the differing
  products.
- `checkback.com` was offered on 9 August 2026 as a premium domain for
  500,000 USD and is therefore not a realistic standard domain:
  <https://checkback.com/>.
- Software, games and entertainment trademark classes can overlap with
  existing CheckBack offerings. A normal web search does not replace a
  professional trademark check.

### Preliminary assessment

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Product fit | very strong |
| Poker authenticity | very strong |
| Pronounceability/memorability | strong |
| Distinctiveness | medium |
| Findability | medium to weak |
| Legal/commercial availability | unresolved, with visible risks |

**Interim conclusion:** CheckBack remains a strong creative candidate, but
should not be decided as the final global brand without a formal availability
check. If the name is too hard to differentiate as a whole product, it is
particularly suitable as the name of the hand replay/analysis area.

## Check before a decision

- Carry out EUIPO, DPMA and, where applicable, UKIPO/USPTO research in the
  relevant trademark classes
- Check the App Store, Google Play, Steam and GitHub for poker, games and
  learning products with the same name
- Check realistic domains including `.poker`, `.game`, `.app`, `.gg` and
  country-specific alternatives
- Check social handles and repository names
- Compare `CheckBack` with at least three more distinctive alternatives using
  the same scoring matrix
- Carry out short pronounceability, recall and association tests with German-
  and English-speaking test persons
- Only after the decision define the migration scope for UI, package metadata,
  repository, app ID, Android namespace and stored data
